DIRECTION SEMANTICS / EXTERNAL_VIEW_V1
UC7.1 defines directions from the external view
The same frozen reference is used in planning language, approval, execution requests and region solving.
R = world-from-external-camera rotation
r = normalize(R[:2, 0])
a = (-r_y, r_x)
right_of = +r       left_of  = -r
behind   = +a       front_of = -a
For these recorded LIBERO scenes:
r ≈ (0, +1); a ≈ (-1, 0).
World +z remains up. Numeric motion targets and region
bounds still use world metres.
right = +r
left = -r
behind = +a
front = -a
anchor
Global horizontal convention, not a perspective Jacobian.
Wrist images never redefine these words.

FRAGMENT CONTRACT
Five reusable measured-motion fragments
Coordinates refer to the robot grip site in world metres; orientation is the hand’s absolute world quaternion (wxyz).
Skill
Semantics
move_to_pose
Absolute world xyz target; quaternion_wxyz or null to retain measured entry orientation.
move_relative
Resolve xyz displacement once from the measured segment-entry pose.
approach
World displacement using the contact profile; a stall is not a contact certificate.
set_gripper
Hold entry pose; open or close as an explicit stage; require pose arrival, minimum duration and
stable measured gap.
wait
Hold the entry pose and command for its declared max_steps, then return.
observation_after
Yield after that segment. Pending stages do not autoplay or resume implicitly after a handoff.

FEEDBACK AND HANDOFF
Return control when execution evidence changes
Local measurements determine completion or yield; they do not decide physical task success.
Event
Measured condition
Pose arrival
Position and rotation within profile tolerances for 2 consecutive samples; acknowledge
completion only after a real action.
Gripper completion
At least 8 open / 12 close actions; 5 recent gaps span ≤0.6 mm; pose arrival also required.
Motion stall
After ≥16 actions, error improves by <0.8 mm over 12 samples. Weighted error = position
error + 0.05 × rotation error.
Maintained-grip gap change
Outside set_gripper while maintaining close: gap rises ≥6 mm above its observed minimum for
3 consecutive samples.
Other handoff boundaries
observation_after, completed wait, step budget or target-envelope rejection. Pending
segments never autoplay.
A stall does not certify contact identity. Pose/gap completion does not certify grasp or placement. Continuation requires a new model decision
from current evidence.
[M1,M3] measured predicates and execution handoff. Thresholds are local fragment rules; they do not impose an episode-wide cap.

TRANSPORT / SUBSCRIPTION ONLY
Request retry is different from task retry
The 180-second deadline covers HTTP open and the full SSE response after authentication.
Condition
Frozen behavior
Overload
server_is_overloaded with HTTP 503 or absent status: at most 2 overload retries.
Transient transport
Missing completion, truncated event, stream interruption or network error: at most 2 retries in
total for this category.
Retry boundary
Same observation, no response envelope and no history commit. Partial output is never
executed.
Independent limits
Overload and transient counters are separate. Mixed errors can exceed 3 total sends; accepted
backoff is at most 60 s.
Non-retryable / task restart
HTTP 520 / unknown_error and deadline exceeded do not retry. A new full attempt needs new
approval and output.
No model downgrade or API-key fallback. t0/t1 recorded zero request errors and zero retries.
